# RECOUP — Synthesized Go-to-Market & Product Brief

RECOUP is a contingency-priced vendor-spend recovery service (30% of recovered cash / 20% of run-rate avoidance) that statelessly analyzes uploaded contracts, invoices and spreadsheets against 14 forensic rules — auto-renewals, escalators, idle SaaS seats, off-contract rates, duplicates, zombie billing, redundant tools, headcount-fee drift — and hands back a ranked, dollar-quantified findings list plus drafted vendor recovery emails. This brief synthesizes four research streams (product, GTM, PE prospecting, Companies House method) into one operating plan.

## The strategic frame
The recovery-audit industry is real, mature and contingency-priced (PRGX, apexanalytix, CBIZ charge 20–40% of recovered funds) but has historically chased the Fortune 1000 on AP duplicate-payment recovery. RECOUP's wedge is two-fold: (1) the **mid-market** — too small for PRGX to bother with, too lean to have a real procurement function; and (2) a **broader forensic surface** — contract economics (escalators, auto-renewals, idle seats, redundant tools) that AP recovery audits skip. Duplicate/erroneous payments are only 0.8–2% of disbursements; the larger leakage lives in the contract-terms layer RECOUP owns. Companies lose ~9% of revenue to ineffective vendor-contract management.

The single best segment is **PE-backed portfolio companies and roll-ups**. They carry an explicit, time-boxed cost-out mandate (the 100-day value-creation plan), the EBITDA-multiple math makes a recurring £1 of cost worth ~8x at exit (£100k recurring saving ≈ £800k of exit value at the 8.4x median PE multiple), the contingency model fits their cash-constrained/no-upfront-spend posture perfectly, and they are vendor-sprawl machines because they grow by bolt-on acquisitions that bolt on duplicate vendor estates. Best of all the buyer is a channel: win one portfolio CFO and the operating-partner team unlocks 40–160 more companies with the identical problem.

## Product: convert detector into a contingency operating system
RECOUP today is a detector trapped in a browser tab. The three build-now picks — **Recovery Tracker**, **CFO Savings Report**, and **Renewal Calendar** — together convert it into an operating system: the Report wins the deal and justifies the 30% invoice, the Calendar drives the avoidance half of the revenue model, and the Tracker is the system of record that lets you actually invoice 30% of what posts. All three are HIGH feasibility, reuse the existing analysis JSON, and need no database (localStorage + JSON/PDF/CSV/.ics export covers persistence). Strong second tier: a macro SaaS-inflation/CPI benchmark overlay, a clause-matched negotiation playbook library, a what-if savings modeler, and a public ROI calculator for lead-gen (ROI calculators convert >40% vs 2–12% for static pages). Explicitly do NOT build per-SKU price benchmarks, live integrations/continuous monitoring, or a two-way vendor dispute portal — all require persistent state and a data moat that breaks RECOUP's stateless, no-DB design.

## GTM in one line
Sell founder-led, signal-triggered outbound to mid-market CFOs (200–2,000 employees, $5M+ vendor spend, no CPO), anchored on PE operating-partner partnerships as the multiplier channel, with "free unless it lands" as the entire competitive advantage. Lead every message with numbers and timeline hooks (timeline hooks get 10.16% reply vs 4.54% for problem hooks; CFOs reply at 7.59%), one persona per sequence, cohorts ≤50, the free-first-look as the demo.

## Prospecting method
Build the list top-down from Companies House: filter the free monthly Company Data CSV by target SIC codes + MEDIUM/FULL/GROUP account category + `NumMortOutstanding ≥ 1` (leverage signal), join the PSC snapshot to flag controllers named *Topco/Midco/Bidco/Holdings* or a known sponsor, then enrich the shortlist via the free REST API (charges to confirm the LBO debenture, officers for decision-makers, XBRL accounts for admin-expense/headcount). Cross-reference with PE firms' public portfolio pages (LDC ~90 holdings, Inflexion, August Equity, NorthEdge, Endless, Modella) and prioritize fresh carve-outs and active roll-ups, where the cost-out mandate is most acute and contracts are being re-baselined now.


## Companies House method

Companies House gives RECOUP a free, top-down way to build a PE-backed mid-market prospect list. The free REST API base URL is https://api.company-information.service.gov.uk (register on the Developer Hub, mint an API key, authenticate via HTTP Basic with the key as username and a blank password). Rate limit: 600 requests per rolling 5-minute window (~2 req/sec sustained; 429 on overage).\n\nKEY ENDPOINTS: GET /advanced-search/companies is the workhorse for ICP filtering (params: company_name_includes/excludes, company_status, company_type/subtype, sic_codes (comma-delimited), incorporated_from/to, location, size, start_index). GET /company/{number} returns status, sic_codes, accounts (category + dates), date_of_creation. GET /company/{number}/persons-with-significant-control surfaces ownership (the PE structure). GET /company/{number}/charges reveals debentures/mortgages (the LBO leverage signal). GET /company/{number}/officers maps decision-makers, and GET /officers/{id}/appointments finds serial PE operating-partner directors across a portfolio. Note: advanced search does NOT filter on account category, charges or PSC directly — filter by SIC + status, then enrich each hit with the profile/charges/PSC calls.\n\nBULK PRODUCTS (for scale): The free monthly Company Data Product CSV (download.companieshouse.gov.uk/en_output.html, all ~5M live companies) is the primary sieve — columns CompanyName, CompanyNumber, CompanyCategory, CompanyStatus, IncorporationDate, RegAddress.* (geo), SICCode.SicText_1..4 (sector), Accounts.AccountCategory (size proxy: FULL/GROUP/MEDIUM/SMALL/MICRO/DORMANT), Mortgages.NumMortOutstanding (leverage), and PreviousName_1..10 (rebrands follow buyouts).
