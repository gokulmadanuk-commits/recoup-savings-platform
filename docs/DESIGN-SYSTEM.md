# Design System — Editorial Finance-Grade

## Palette

| Token | Hex | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paper / Ivory | `#F5F1E8` | Primary page background - the warm 'paper off-white'. ~60% of every screen. |
| Bone | `#EDE7D9` | Background alt - alternating sections, card insets, subtle banding, low-contrast zebra rows. |
| Forest Green | `#1F3A2E` | Primary dark - hero blocks, footer, full-bleed feature panels, primary CTA on paper, chart primary series. The 'deep forest'. ~30% of layout. |
| Pine (Forest Deep) | `#16271F` | Darkest green - footers, gradient floors, image overlays, stat-card backgrounds, tooltip backgrounds. |
| Apollo Emerald | `#008168` | Mid accent - links, active nav underline, chart secondary series, kickers, small accents. Secondary to forest, never a large fill. |
| Verdant | `#2E7D5B` | Bright green - hover states on green, secondary data series, tags, and positive/gain figures (up). |
| Near-black Ink | `#1A1A17` | Body copy and headlines on ivory - a warm near-black, never pure #000. |
| Slate Ink | `#4A4A42` | Muted ink - secondary text, captions, table sub-labels, axis labels. |
| Muted Gold / Antique Brass | `#B08D45` | Seasoning accent - hairline rules, table-header bottom border, italic accent words, premium markers, chart tertiary. Used sparingly, like a fountain-pen flourish, never a large fill. |
| Champagne Sand | `#D8C9A8` | Tag fills, subtle dividers, chart area fills, disabled states on dark. |
| Cream | `#F0E9D8` | Text, wordmark, and cream pill buttons on forest backgrounds. |
| Terracotta | `#A6492F` | Negative figures (down), losses - earthy, deliberately not alarm-red. |

## Typography

- **Display:** Fraunces (variable; opsz ~144 at large sizes, SOFT/WONK axes; weight 400-500 for elegance, 600 for impact). The closest free analog to Apollo's modified-Garamond editorial feel. Use true italics for accent words inside headlines, e.g. 'Built for the next generation of allocators.'
- **Body:** Newsreader (on-screen reading serif, premium-magazine feel; its italic carries pull-quotes and the lead/standfirst). Spectral or Source Serif 4 are acceptable alternates. UI/labels/data/eyebrows use Inter at small sizes only.

Three-role system: Fraunces (display) + Newsreader (body) + Inter (UI/labels/data). All Google-hosted. Scale (1.25-ish, tightened at display): Display XL hero clamp(2.75rem,6vw,5.5rem)/1.02 tracking -0.02em; H1 3rem/1.05 -0.015em; H2 2.25rem/1.1; H3 Fraunces 600 1.5rem/1.2; Lead Newsreader 400 italic 1.375rem/1.5; Body Newsreader 400 1.125rem/1.65; Caption 0.9375rem/1.55; Eyebrow Inter 600 0.75rem uppercase tracking 0.14em; Button/nav Inter 500 0.9375rem tracking 0.02em; Data Inter 500 with tabular-nums. Rules: headlines get tight leading (1.0-1.1) and negative tracking; body gets generous leading (1.6-1.7); emphasis via serif italics, not bold; cap body measure at 62-70ch (max-w-[42rem]); ALWAYS font-feature-settings tabular-nums + lining-nums on financial figures so columns align like a printed prospectus.

## Components

BUTTONS (signature = cream pill with arrow): inline-flex items-center gap-2 rounded-full px-6 py-3 text-sm tracking-wide transition-all duration-300. On forest/dark: bg-cream text-forest, trailing arrow that translates right ~4px on hover, bg shifts toward #FFFFFF with faint shadow. On paper: bg-forest text-cream, hover -> bg-emerald or darken to #16271F. Secondary/ghost: transparent, 1px ink-or-cream border, pill, label only. Tertiary: ink/emerald label with a gold (#B08D45) underline that grows in on hover. No harsh shadows. CARDS: bg paper or bone, rounded-xl (12-16px, soft not pill), border 1px solid rgba(26,26,23,0.08) (hairline ink) instead of shadow; hover lift translate-y-[-2px] + border darken + faint shadow-[0_8px_30px_rgba(31,58,46,0.06)]. Insight/finding cards: uppercase Inter kicker in emerald, Fraunces H3 title, Newsreader excerpt, gold hairline <hr> above a 'Read ->' / 'View finding ->' link; optional muted image with slight green duotone. Stat cards on forest.deep: cream text, large Fraunces figure with tabular-nums, gold thin rule under the label. TABLES (finance, the core surface): no vertical gridlines; horizontal 1px hairlines only rgba(26,26,23,0.10); header row Inter 600 uppercase 0.75rem tracked text-ink-muted with a 1px gold (#B08D45) bottom border; body figures Inter tabular-nums right-aligned; optional bone zebra at very low contrast; positive figures verdant (#2E7D5B), negative terracotta (#A6492F); generous py-4 rows - let it breathe like a printed prospectus. NAV: transparent over hero (cream wordmark on forest, ink on paper), Inter 500 with generous tracking, active/hover gold-or-emerald underline animating in (2px width transition); on scroll condense to solid paper/95 backdrop-blur with bottom hairline; right-aligned cream pill CTA ('Client Login ->'). CHARTS (in-palette, never default blue): series forest #1F3A2E -> emerald #008168 -> gold #B08D45 -> verdant #2E7D5B, sand #D8C9A8 for area fills (8-12% opacity); paper backgrounds; gridlines faint ink hairlines rgba(26,26,23,0.06) or omitted; axis labels Inter small ink-muted; tooltips forest.deep bg with cream text, rounded; thin strokes 1.5-2px, flat, no glossy gradients. TEXTURES (tactile premium layer, felt-not-seen): global SVG feTurbulence fractal-noise grain at 3-5% opacity over paper and forest panels via a fixed .grain::before pseudo-element (content:'';position:fixed;inset:0;pointer-events:none;z-index:50;opacity:.04); optional faint cream/gold curved line-art SVG in the hero at 6-10% opacity, large and asymmetric, behind the headline.

## Principles

Editorial finance-grade, a private-markets annual report rendered for the web - explicitly NOT a white-and-blue SaaS dashboard. Discipline: 60% paper/ivory ground, 30% forest green anchors (heroes, footer, feature bands), 10% ink + gold accents; gold is a seasoning, never a fill across large areas. Whitespace is the luxury signal: max-w-7xl container, px-6 md:px-12 lg:px-20, section rhythm py-24 to py-32. Asymmetry over centered symmetry: off-axis headlines, content on ~7 columns with whitespace ballast, oversized Fraunces/gold section indices ('01 - Strategy'). Alternating paper -> forest full-bleed -> paper bands create a magazine-spread cadence; footer always forest.deep with cream wordmark and gold hairline dividers. Hairline gold/ink rules separate sections instead of heavy boxes. Imagery muted and desaturated with a green duotone so photos sit inside the palette. Motion restrained and confident: entrances fade + 12-20px rise, 500-700ms, ease-out cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1), stagger children ~80ms; micro-interactions (arrow nudge 4px, underlines grow from left, cards lift 2px) at duration-300; no bounce, no parallax; always respect prefers-reduced-motion (disable transforms); if built as an Astro lead-gen surface keep client JS at zero and do entrances/hovers in pure CSS (@starting-style / scroll-driven animations). Numbers set like a printed prospectus, one literary serif speaking in italics, and air everywhere.
